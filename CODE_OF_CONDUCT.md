# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

V2UI contributors and maintainers commit to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Be considerate, give actionable feedback, respect differing viewpoints, and keep discussions focused on the project. Protect user privacy: never share private review recordings, manifests, credentials, or source code without authorization.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the contact options on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies in all project spaces and whenever someone officially represents V2UI.
